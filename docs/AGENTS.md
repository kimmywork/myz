# Document Guide

- `knowledges/` contains some **GROUND TRUTH** - standards, implementation references, third party apis etc.
- `legal/` contains some legal documents of this suite, including term of services and privacy policy etc.
- `track/` contains requirements and delivery plans for features.
- `prompts/` (deprecated) were intented to be a historical archive of prompts to agents, now deprecated.
- `research/` are some feature discovery documents including competitive products research and theory validation.
- `archives/` archived documents, ignore them.
